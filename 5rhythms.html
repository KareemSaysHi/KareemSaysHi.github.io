New 5Rhythms Each Day!

Every day at 6am, come back to see this playlist update! Spotify generates a 30-40 minute playlist, always beginning with 5 minutes of silence and ending with 3, and selecting a song in each of the 5Rhythms categories (Flowing, Staccato, Chaos, Lyrical, Stillness) by choosing songs that are similar to those in the playlists below (scroll down to see them).
